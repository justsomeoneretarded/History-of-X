# Historical image licenses

These reviewed images retain the individual licenses recorded in manifest.json, including creator, original source URL and license URL. Public-domain images remain public domain. CC BY and CC BY-SA images retain their stated licenses; attribution is required.

The snapshots were resized to a maximum of 2400 pixels and converted to JPEG. The video renderer additionally crops, grades and animates them, and includes full attribution in its visible end credits and sources-and-credits file.

These media files are separate from the program code. Refer to each original source record for provenance and licensing details.
