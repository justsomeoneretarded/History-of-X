# Historical media

`ethiopia-1935-1.mp4` and `ethiopia-1935-2.mp4` are silent excerpts from Universal Newsreel's **25 Years Ago (1960/09/22)**, distributed in the Internet Archive Universal Newsreels collection with a public-domain designation:

- Source: https://archive.org/details/1960-09-22_25_years_ago
- Metadata/license record: https://archive.org/metadata/1960-09-22_25_years_ago
- Segments: 98.0–102.5 seconds and 124.0–128.5 seconds.
- The content depicts 1935. The film uses these as a separately dated, wordless 1935 bridge; they are never described as footage of Adwa in 1896.
- Original newsreel audio is removed. SHA-256 checksums and modifications are recorded in `catalog.json`.

`ethiopian-gospel-1440.jpg` is the Cleveland Museum of Art's Open Access photograph of **Single Leaf from a Gospel Book with a Portrait of Saint Luke**, Ethiopia, c. 1440–80 (1999.212). The museum API identifies it as CC0:

- Object: https://clevelandart.org/art/1999.212
- API: https://openaccess-api.clevelandart.org/api/artworks/160940
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Metadata and checksum: `photographs.json`.

Other genuine historical portraits are selected from the public-domain records already bundled in `assets/presets/ethiopia/manifest.json`. The final package lists the images actually used in `media-credits.json` and the upload description. Credits are kept outside the picture.
